EYE CANDY

Five pages whose only brief is: be beautiful. No simulation to solve, no mechanic to win, no layout to audit — a shader, a subject and a grade each. Plain static files, three.js 0.170.0 from a pinned CDN importmap, no build step, no npm, no server. Every one carries a control panel, a quality preset, interaction hints and a headless-Chromium check. ← the lab hub · the experiments →

1 · Aurora

Eleven additive light curtains, each a domain-warped fBm body cut by ridged rays, over a procedural night sky with three star layers and a dust-noised Milky Way. The lake is a real planar mirror — the world is rendered a second time from below and sampled into the ice with ripples, frost, pressure cracks and a specular path from the moon.

2 · Iridescence

The colour is physics, not a texture: optical path length through a film of refractive index 1.333 divided by the wavelength of each channel drives a cosine. The film drains downward and swirls, Fresnel makes the rim a mirror and the face almost invisible, and a click burns a hole through it and leaves droplets.

3 · Nebula

200 000 points (up to 300 000) placed and pushed entirely in the vertex shader from a per-particle seed: shell distribution, three arms, differential spin that shears them, three value-noise taps that pull the dust into filaments, a cosine palette by radius and density. The CPU updates nothing but time.

4 · Bioluminescence

Jellyfish lit by themselves. A lathe-built bell clamps shut on a wave travelling from apex to margin; five frilled arms and twenty tentacles are ribbons placed in the vertex shader where each segment lags the one above, so the trail follows the pulse. Shafts fall from a surface far above and marine snow drifts through them.

5 · Kaleidoscope

A generator feeding a feedback loop, stored in wedge space so that rotating and zooming the afterimage are just a texture lookup: shift the angle, scale the radius, multiply by a fade, add this frame's domain-warped plasma and voronoi facets. One wedge is computed; the display pass mirrors it n times.
