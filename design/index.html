Eleven design briefs, built and measured

Every page in this folder was built by the same model — Swift 1.5 Flash-Next (strata/swift-1.5-flash-next-iq3_s, the IQ3_S quant of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, 131K context, served by Strata on a home GPU box) working as the agent pi — from a written brief, as plain static files with no build step, screenshotted in headless Chromium, inspected against its own screenshots, then fixed and re-shot. Each folder carries a NOTES.md with a self-critique, a list of remaining weak points, and ratings out of five. This page is the summary: what was made, what the numbers say, and where the work is weak.

The one thing this folder could not grade itself

Every task rated its own visual quality 4 out of 5; eleven 4/5s is a prior, not a measurement, and this folder's own Limits says the self-graded axes are its least useful numbers. So the frames were measured instead: node tools/liveliness.mjs --per-task screenshots each brief at 1440 and reads the pixels — Hasler–Süsstrunk colourfulness, hue families, ink coverage, tonal entropy, panel depth. One tile per brief, painted in the background colour measured from that frame and coloured in by its measured colourfulness: this band is the data, not decoration.

What every task had to be

  • Plain static files, relative paths, no build step — GitHub Pages compatible. Fonts only from pinned @fontsource packages on a CDN, or vendored. TypeScript or shell, no Python.
  • A real subject with real constraints, invented but consistent: a map app, a depot control tower, an offline birding log, a terminal tool, a design system, a hydrographic firm, an estuary's flood data, a grant application, a motion system, a flood portal, one page in nine styles.
  • Screenshots desktop and phone, plus the states that matter (empty, error, offline, filtered, reduced motion, dark, print), through a RAM-capped headless Chromium: tools/shoot.mjs.
  • One round of self-critique at least, against the screenshots, with the fixes stated as numbers that moved.
  • Ratings out of five on five axes, and a section listing what is still wrong.

Verdict on range and taste

The harness, and the time it was blind

Every number on this page that is not an opinion came from tools/: shoot.mjs (screenshots plus the audit), rendered.mjs (the text a browser rendered, and computed styles), overflow.mjs (which element is wider than the phone), and one measure.mjs per task that needs its own measurement (the system's contrast pairs, the motion audit, the redesign's before/after diff, the style study's content parity).

The audit ran per element and skipped any element that had children. Emphasis lives in inline elements — the em in a headline, the strong in a paragraph — so the coloured words that carry the meaning were exactly what it never read; and it parsed rgba(0, 0, 0, 0) as black, so gradient-clipped text was measured as a colour it does not have. Task 11's before/after pair is the proof: the glass page with a gradient headline passed with zero findings under the old rule and reports one under the new one. shoot.mjs now walks text nodes and reports a near-transparent foreground as unmeasurable. Contrast counts for tasks 01–10 were produced by the element rule and are looser than the ones for 11.

A tool that clears 40 shots and cannot see the loudest failure on the page is the main lesson of this folder: the measurements are worth keeping only when you can see what they measure.

Limits of the whole exercise

  • No readers. Every subject is invented. No one was interviewed, no task was observed, no analytics were consulted. The measurements are real and the users are not.
  • No assistive technology, no second browser, no second machine. Chromium 152, software rendering, one Linux box. Names, landmarks, roles, focus rings and contrast are measured; being read is not.
  • Self-assessment. The ratings, the "remaining weak points" and the verdict on this page are all written by the thing being judged. The scores are the least reliable numbers here.
  • Coverage is the manifest. The audit only measures the shots the manifest asks for. 04-tui had nine shots, every one of them wide, and at 390 px its page scrolled sideways by 282 px — a fact nobody knew because nobody had asked for 390. 06-identity had a phone shot and 63 px of overflow that the report printed and nobody read. Both measure 0 px now, 04-tui has two 390 px shots, and every task's shots/ folder is a page carrying its own audit numbers. A width that is never requested is a width that is never measured.
  • No frontier arm. The verdict's comparison with a strong frontier model is a judgement against expectation, not a measured A/B: no Claude-4/Opus-, GPT-5- or Gemini-2.5-Pro-class page was built in this repo. The one measured neighbour is whole-repo-qa/README.md — this model against the router's dense 27B on 100K-token whole-repo Q&A. The briefs, the manifest and the harness are the shape of a real A/B; the second arm is not here.
  • One drop. A twelfth brief — four conflicting-constraint problems with written decision records (dense data at AA on a 390 px phone; a brand palette that fails contrast; a playful brand in a clinical form; one console for beginners and experts) — was dropped by the owner before it was built. Trade-off reasoning is present inside the eleven tasks (each NOTES.md has a round-1→round-2 and a weights section), but it was never isolated and rated as its own exercise.
