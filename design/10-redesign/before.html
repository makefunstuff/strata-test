Flood warning — A Flood Warning is in force for Designated Area HE-07 (Reference: TID/2024/1187) comprising the designated coastal flood zone of Harrowmouth, Lower Sandbar and Upcerne at and above 4.20 m AOD. Responsible persons are advised to implement their Flood Risk Management Plan and to make application to the Authority in respect of any temporary defences not already installed. Duration of the warning is indefinite pending review of the Estuary Gauge Array and the Met Office Nautilus model run.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Am I in a flood zone? A: Designated areas are defined in the Designated Areas Order 2019 and are depicted on the indicative map, available from the document library.

Q: What should I do in a flood? A: Refer to your Flood Risk Management Plan and contact the relevant emergency services in the event of a danger to life.

Q: Is a tide gauge calibrated? A: Gauges are verified annually against the Ordnance Datum network pursuant to the Authority's Determination.
