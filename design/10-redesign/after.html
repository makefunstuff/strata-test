Flood warning in force

Harrowmouth, Lower Sandbar and Upcerne

The tide is expected to rise above the flood line of 4.20 metres between 21:30 on 3 December and 06:12 on 5 December. The forecast peak is 4.61 m at 22:14 on 3 December — 41 cm above the flood line.

What to do

  1. Put the flood barriers up, and move insurance documents and your medicines to somewhere dry and high.
  2. Turn the gas off at the meter if water is coming in. Do not touch the meter if it is already wet.
  3. Tell a neighbour who may not have seen this, especially one who lives alone.
  4. Do not drive through flood water, and do not walk it. Fifteen centimetres of moving water can knock you over.

Tide times, Harrowmouth

Heights are in metres above Chart Datum, as determined in 2011. Times are UTC; add an hour in British Summer Time. A row is tinted amber when its tide reaches the flood line of 4.20 m. These times are for planning, not for navigation.

Help with the words on this page

Chart Datum
The height the tide tables use: the lowest level a tide is expected to reach. It is not the datum used before 2011, so a height from an older document is not comparable with one on this page.
Flood line
The height at which water starts to reach properties at this gauge. Every gauge has its own. Harrowmouth's is 4.20 m.
Designated area
A place named in the Designated Areas Order 2019. Being in one changes what you must do, and what you can claim.
Spring range
The difference between the highest high and the lowest low over the spring tides around that date. A bigger number means a bigger tide.

The heights on this page come from the twelve gauges of the Estuary Gauge Array and from the Nautilus model run. A gauge with no reading shows a gap, never a guess. How the gauges are verified.
