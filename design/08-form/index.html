    Step 1 · Eligibility

    Apply for a Property Flood Resilience grant

    We pay up to £5,000 towards fittings that keep water out of a home that has flooded, or that let it dry out fast. We will ask four short questions, then the property, then the works.

    Before you start

    • You must own the home, or have the owner's written permission.
    • You need a grid reference or a postcode — the address lookup takes the grid reference.
    • Two quotes are needed for works over £1,500.
    • This form takes about 8 minutes and saves as you go.
    Do you own this property?

    If you rent, you can still apply with the owner's written permission.

    You cannot apply for this grant as a tenant without permission. The owner must consent in writing. Ask us for the permission letter and come back to this form.

    Has this property flooded in the last 5 years?

    From any source: river, tide, surface water or groundwater.

    If it has not flooded

    This grant pays for resilience after a flood. For prevention only, the Estuary Risk fund pays up to £1,200 and has a shorter form. You can carry on here if you expect flooding this winter.
