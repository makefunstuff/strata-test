An identity you can
draw with a ruler

Meridian surveys coastlines and sells the charts. The mark is one circle, one horizon, one rising line and three radii. It is the same drawing at 512 px and at 16 px, and the 32 icons in it are built on the 24 px grid with a 1.5 stroke that does not fill in when it is shrunk.

01

Construction

The mark is drawn, not traced. Every point sits on a radius of 6, 12, 18 or 24, on the horizon at y = 30, or on the 15° meridian. The grid is the legal evidence; the mark is what is left when you remove it.

The grid48 × 48, 8 divisions
0 0 48 48the field; every construction point is an integer
r 6 · 12 · 18 · 24four concentric circles; the ring is r 18, the sun is r 6
y 30the horizon, at five eighths of the field, so the sky outweighs the sea
15°the meridian, tilted once, which is the whole idea: a line that knows where it is
No curve in the mark has a radius outside that set. If a change cannot be stated in these numbers, it is not a change to the mark.
What the mark carriesreading order
01
A ring, open at the bottom

A survey instrument's field of view, and a horizon that does not close: the coast continues past the frame.

02
A horizon at five eighths

Below the line is water (the field), above is sky (the reading). The line is the product.

03
A meridian, tilted 15°

The longitude that fixes a chart. Tilted, because a true meridian on a Mercator sheet is a straight vertical — the tilt is the observation, not the map.

04
A sun at the intersection

Rising out of the horizon, at the point where the meridian crosses it. One dot, r 6, in signal red — the only colour in the mark.

Test it as a brand should be tested: read it at arm's length, then at 16 px, then in one colour, then embroidered. If it survives all four it is a mark, not an illustration.
02

Lockups

Five lockups, no more. The name is set in Instrument Serif with the final s left unpointed; the mark never touches the type, the clear space is one sun-diameter (12 units) on every side.

03

Scale & minimum

The mark is drawn once and shrunk honestly: 256, 96, 48, 32, 24, 16 px at 1:1. Below 16 the sun disappears at a stroke width below one device pixel, so the favicon uses the reduced mark — ring, horizon, meridian, no sun.

12
Clear space is one sun-diameter on all four sides, measured from the ring, not the box. Nothing — type, rule, photograph edge, the other lockup — enters it. A mark with a crowd around it is not a mark, it is furniture.
04

One colour

The mark has to work in one ink: etched on brass, screen-printed on a canvas bag, embroidered on a cap, stamped on a crate, cut from vinyl at 20 mm. Red is a preference, never a requirement.

The 1-bit versions drop the sun's disc and keep its ring: a solid dot at 3 mm of embroidery becomes a black hole in the middle of the mark. The stencil cuts the horizon where the meridian crosses it, so the crossing reads on a rough surface.
05

The 24 px grid

Every icon lives in a 24 × 24 box with a 2 px gutter, a 20 px live area, a 1.5 stroke, round caps and joins, and a 30° step for every diagonal. Two icons are shown with the grid on: the compass rose and the tide clock.

Keylines24 box · 20 live · 1.5 stroke
The rules that make a set, not a pile6 rules
  • One stroke, always 1.5. Weight is not an emphasis tool in an icon set; a bold icon reads as a mistake at 16 px.
  • Round caps and joins, 2 px gutter. Shapes stop 2 px from the box edge so a row of icons reads as a row, not a fence.
  • Diagonals in 30° steps. Free angles break the pixel grid at 16 px and the set looks drunk.
  • Optical, not mathematical, centring. The compass rose is drawn 0.25 px high: a 24-circle is optically small in a 24 box.
  • 16 px is a second drawing. Stroke 1.25, the two innermost details dropped — the 16 px row is not a scale, it is an edit.
  • An icon never carries the only meaning. Every icon in the product has a label beside it; icons are signposts, not the sentence.

    Files

    What ships, and the size each file is allowed to be the answer to.

    favicon.svg16–32 px in a browser tab. The reduced mark: ring, horizon, meridian, no sun, stroke 3 for a sub-pixel-safe line.
    mark.svgThe full mark, 48 × 48, one file, currentColor + one var(--signal). Every other file is derived from this one.
    lockup.svgHorizontal lockup with the word set as outlines, so a PDF of a survey report never falls back to a system serif.
    icons.svgOne sprite, 32 symbols, <use> by name. 5.5 kB, 1.9 kB gzipped; every icon on this page is drawn from the same data.
    appicon.svg512, 180, 96, 48: the ring and sun on ink, with the safe-area grid the stores demand.
    identity.cssThe tokens the identity needs: ink, paper, sea, signal, and the two type roles. Nothing else is a brand decision.
