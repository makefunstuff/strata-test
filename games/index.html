Strata Games

Five three.js games, each written by a headless pi agent running Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (IQ2_XS) on Strata, from one short spec prompt. Complexity rises from 1 to 5. Click a game to play it.

1 · Breakout

Simple: paddle, ball, bricks. ←/→ to move, Space to start.

2 · Endless runner

Arcade: three lanes, rising speed. ←/→ lanes, Space jumps.

3 · Marble maze

Physics: cannon-es, shadows. Arrows/WASD tilt the board.

4 · Tower defense

Strategy: pathfinding enemies, waves. Click tiles to build, N for the next wave.

5 · Flight

Showcase: procedural terrain, fog, sky, particles, bloom. Arrows/WASD fly, Shift boosts.
How they were made, the stats and the ratings: games/README.md.
