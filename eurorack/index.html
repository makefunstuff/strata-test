EURORACK

A modular synth in the browser. It makes sound now — press keys ZXVHNJ or tap the keys strip. Plug a real MIDI controller if you have one.

  • Patch: drag from an output jack to an input jack. One output can feed many inputs; click a cable to unplug it.
  • Build: ＋ modules, then click or drag into the rails. Drag a module by its name strip; ✕ removes it.
  • Knobs: drag up/down, or mouse wheel; shift for fine, double-click to reset, click a segmented knob to cycle.
  • Sequencer: drag a step up/down for pitch, click to toggle its gate. It free-runs at the ♩ tempo unless you patch a clock into CLK.
  • Music out of the box: the presets menu has five finished patches — Acid Groove, Pluck & Space, Deep Drone, Ambient Garden, Kraut Motor.
  • Keep: save/patches (localStorage), exp/imp (JSON), share puts the whole patch in the URL, ● rec bounces a WAV.

Output is limited and safe. If you hear nothing, click anywhere once (browsers need a tap before audio).
